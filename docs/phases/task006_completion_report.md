## 6. Financial semantics

See `docs/data/financial_aggregation_policy.md`. In summary:

- All totals go through `compassio_ml.finance.aggregate`.
- Metrics are single-source with one flow kind, level, currency and price basis.
- Missing values are counted, never zero.
- Multi-country and regional values are excluded from totals and counted.
- Pledges are separate from commitments.
- ADB repayments are labelled "not funding" (`is_funding: false`).
- CFU instrument components are never added to totals.
- `gcf_financing` is always `unavailable`: the export states no currency, unit or date.
- No currency conversion exists.
